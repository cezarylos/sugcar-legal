SugCar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-27

1. Overview

SugCar is a secondary glucose data visualization app. It is designed for informational, glanceable awareness and is not a medical device.

2. Data We Process

SugCar processes account authentication details and glucose-related readings required to display your dashboard (for example: value, trend arrow, range status, and last updated time).

3. How SugCar Uses Data (Pass-Through Model)

SugCar acts primarily as a pass-through visualization layer for data retrieved from LibreLinkUp-compatible services.

  • Credentials are used to authenticate requests.
  • Credentials are not stored permanently on external SugCar servers.
  • Backend session/auth data is kept in volatile memory only for active operation and is cleared on logout/session end.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

SugCar does not sell health data. SugCar does not share health data with advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

SugCar connects to third-party glucose data services (for example, LibreLinkUp APIs) strictly to retrieve data requested by the user.

Use of third-party platforms is also governed by those providers’ own terms and privacy policies.

6. Security

  • Data transmission is intended to occur over secure transport (HTTPS) in production deployments.
  • Sensitive app-side values are stored using platform security features (for example, iOS Keychain where applicable).
  • SugCar is built to minimize retention of sensitive credentials and health data.

7. Children’s Privacy

SugCar is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete such data.

8. Data Retention

SugCar retains only the minimum data needed for current operation. Operational caches may be temporary and may be deleted on logout, app reset, or service restart.

9. International Use

If you use SugCar from outside your home jurisdiction, your data may be processed where your selected service infrastructure is hosted.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

11. Contact
