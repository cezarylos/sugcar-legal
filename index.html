Privacy Policy

SugCar is a secondary glucose data visualization app designed for informational, glanceable awareness and is not a medical device.

Data Processing Model

SugCar acts as a secondary visualization layer for data retrieved from LibreLinkUp-compatible services.

  • Credentials are used only to authenticate requests to retrieve glucose data.
  • For active service operation, credentials and session tokens may be stored on SugCar backend systems in encrypted form at rest.
  • SugCar uses encrypted storage controls and access restrictions to limit exposure of sensitive account data.
  • Users can disconnect/logout, which invalidates the active SugCar app session.

No Sale / No Ad Sharing

SugCar does not sell health data and does not share health data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children’s Privacy

SugCar is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
