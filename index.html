Privacy Policy

SugCar is a secondary glucose data visualization app designed for informational, glanceable awareness and is not a medical device.

Data Processing Model

SugCar connects directly to your personal Nightscout server to retrieve glucose data. All communication occurs between your device and your Nightscout instance.

  • Credentials: Your Nightscout URL and API secret are stored locally on your device in the iOS Keychain (encrypted, accessible after first device unlock) and in a shared App Group container so background features can function.
  • No backend servers: SugCar does not operate any backend infrastructure. Your credentials and glucose data are never transmitted to SugCar servers.
  • On-device storage only: Glucose readings, thresholds, display units, and user preferences (voice settings, theme) are stored locally using iOS UserDefaults and Keychain.
  • Users can disconnect/logout at any time, which deletes all stored credentials from both Keychain and shared storage.

Voice & Speech Features

  • SugCar includes an optional text-to-speech feature that reads glucose values aloud using Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer.
  • All speech synthesis happens entirely on-device. No audio or text is transmitted externally.
  • Users can enable or disable voice, choose a voice, adjust speech rate, and configure what information is spoken (value, trend, time, unit).

Siri & Shortcuts Integration

  • SugCar registers an App Shortcut ("Check My Blood Sugar") via the App Intents framework.
  • When triggered via Siri or the Shortcuts app, the intent fetches the latest reading directly from your Nightscout server in the background and returns a spoken response via Siri.
  • No data is shared with Apple beyond what Siri processes to deliver the spoken dialog.

Live Activities & Dynamic Island

  • SugCar uses iOS Live Activities to display glucose readings on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • Live Activity data is kept on-device and updated from your Nightscout server.
  • Tapping a Live Activity opens the app and triggers a data refresh.

No Sale / No Ad Sharing

SugCar does not sell health data and does not share health data with advertisers or data brokers.

No Analytics or Tracking

SugCar does not include any analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or third-party tracking.

Children's Privacy

SugCar is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
