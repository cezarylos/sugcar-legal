## 4. Voice & Speech Features
SugCar includes an optional text-to-speech feature that reads glucose values aloud using Apple's on-device `AVSpeechSynthesizer`. Spoken glucose readings are for convenience and glanceable awareness only. They do not replace visual verification with a primary BGM.

## 5. Siri & Shortcuts
SugCar registers an App Shortcut ("Check My Blood Sugar") via the App Intents framework. When triggered via Siri or the Shortcuts app, the intent fetches the latest reading directly from your Nightscout server and returns a spoken response via Siri. Spoken responses are for informational awareness only and do not constitute medical advice.

## 6. Live Activities & Dynamic Island
SugCar uses iOS Live Activities to display glucose readings on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Live Activity data is for informational awareness only. Tapping a Live Activity opens the app and may trigger a data refresh and spoken reading.
